01 — The Craft

Planned backward
from the aisle.

Wedding skin cannot be crammed the week before — it is sequenced. Facials peak on the right week, brow work heals on schedule, the body polish lands days out, and nothing new is ever tried close to the date. Your plan is written to your calendar the day you book.

Just as deliberately, the experience is a refuge from planning itself: one artist, one quiet room, tea afterward, and a standing appointment that no vendor, seating chart, or group text can enter. Many brides call it the sanest hour of their engagement.

03 — Questions

Asked often,
answered honestly.

A personalized program at Aileen's Beauty in Campbell, CA sequencing facials, brow perfection, lip blush if desired, and full-body polish across the months before your wedding — planned backward from your date by one artist in a private, women-only studio.

Ideally three to six months before the wedding — enough time for facial results to compound and any brow or lip work to fully heal and settle. A shorter runway still helps; the consultation builds the best plan for the time available.

The studio serves one client at a time by design, so treatments are individual — but wedding-party members are welcome to book their own appointments in the countdown. Many mothers of the bride claim the La Mer facial.

Anything new within three weeks of the date — new actives, first-time treatments, experiments. Your program deliberately front-loads anything unfamiliar and keeps the final stretch to proven, calming rituals.

Call or text (650) 305-8036 with your wedding date. The studio at 816 W Hamilton Ave, Campbell, CA serves brides from San Jose, Los Gatos, Saratoga, and across the Bay Area — by appointment only.
