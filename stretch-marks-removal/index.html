01 — The Craft

Goodbye to stretch marks,
and to the fear.

Our mission is clear: say goodbye to stretch marks and fear. Treatment begins with truth — white marks and red marks respond differently, and age, depth, and skin tone all matter. Your consultation maps exactly what improvement your skin can honestly expect.

The work itself is gradual and non-laser: stimulating the skin's own renewal so texture smooths and contrast fades over a course of sessions. Every appointment is private, unhurried, and women-only — because this is skin most clients have never shown a stranger.

03 — Questions

Asked often,
answered honestly.

Honestly: stretch marks can be dramatically softened and blurred, though no treatment anywhere can guarantee complete erasure. At Aileen's Beauty in Campbell, CA the consultation tells you what your specific marks — by age, color, and depth — can realistically achieve before you book a course.

The treatment stimulates the skin's own renewal in the marked area, encouraging smoother texture and softer color contrast over a series of sessions — without laser heat, which can be risky on some skin tones.

Most courses run between three and eight sessions spaced several weeks apart, depending on the maturity and depth of the marks. Newer, red-toned marks generally respond faster than older white ones.

Sensation is mild and numbing is available. Expect temporary redness in the treated area for a day or two; normal clothing and activities resume immediately.
