01 — The Method

Removal without laser.
Precision without compromise.

Laser shatters pigment with heat. We take a gentler path — saline eyebrow tattoo removal with the PhiRemoval technique, drawing pigment up and out through the surface of the skin, session by session, the way it was placed.

01

The Consultation

We begin with your story. Under magnification, we read the pigment — its depth, its undertone, how your skin has held it — and map a removal plan that is honest about timeline and outcome.

02

The Gentle Lift

Using the PhiRemoval saline technique, pigment is drawn upward through the skin's surface — no heat, no shattering, no risk to the follicle. Your natural brow hair remains untouched, growing as it always has.

03

The Quiet Healing

A fine healing veil forms and releases within seven to ten days, carrying pigment with it. You leave with a complete aftercare ritual; your skin leaves with instructions to rest.

04

The Reveal

Session by session — most clients need two to six — the old work fades and your own brow re-emerges. A clean canvas, ready to be left beautifully bare or redesigned the right way.

02 — A Considered Choice

Why we chose not to use a laser.

For body tattoos, laser is often right. The brow is different — delicate skin, precious hair, and cosmetic pigments that lasers misread.

The Aileen's Method

Saline · PhiRemoval · Non-Laser
  • Lifts pigment out through the skin's surface — no heat trauma
  • Safe for every pigment color, including warm, red & flesh tones
  • Protects natural brow hair and follicles completely
  • Suitable for all skin tones, including deeper complexions
  • Visible lightening after every session
  • Performed slowly, by hand, by a licensed esthetician

Laser Removal

High-Heat Pigment Shattering
  • Shatters pigment with concentrated heat in delicate brow skin
  • Can oxidize warm & titanium-based pigments darker instead of lighter
  • Heat can singe brow hair and damage follicles
  • Higher risk of hypopigmentation on deeper skin tones
  • Pigment particles are processed through the lymphatic system
  • Built for body ink — not for cosmetic brow pigments

05 — Your Journey

Honest timelines.
No surprises.

Microblading removal done properly is a process, not an event. Here is exactly how it unfolds — because trust is built on knowing what comes next.

Day 0

Consultation & first session

Pigment analysis, a candid conversation about your expected number of sessions, and — if your skin is ready — your first gentle lift the same day.

Days 1–10

The healing veil

Mild redness settles within hours; most clients return to their day immediately. A fine protective veil forms and releases naturally, carrying pigment with it.

Weeks 2–8

Rest & regeneration

Your skin completes its renewal cycle while pigment continues to lighten. We schedule your next session only when your skin is truly ready — around eight weeks.

Sessions 2–6

The gradual reveal

Each visit lifts another layer of history. Depth, color, and skin type set the pace — we never rush what should be done gently.

After

A clean canvas

Wear your natural brow with nothing at all — or let us redesign it properly with fine, hair-like microblading strokes once your skin has fully settled.

09 — Questions

Asked, answered.

Laser works by shattering pigment with heat — which can singe natural brow hair, turn warm or titanium-based pigments darker, and carries a higher risk of scarring and hypopigmentation on delicate brow skin. Our saline-based method gently lifts pigment out through the surface instead. It works on all pigment colors, protects your follicles, and is safe for all skin tones.

Most clients need between two and six sessions, spaced roughly eight weeks apart. The honest answer depends on how deeply the original pigment was implanted, its color, and your skin type — which is exactly what we assess at your consultation. You will see lightening after every single session.

Comfort is central to the experience. Most clients describe the sensation as similar to — or milder than — their original microblading appointment. Expect mild redness or sensitivity afterward; most clients return to normal activities the same day.

No — protecting your natural brows is precisely why we chose a non-laser technique. Pigment is lifted from the skin without affecting the hair follicle, so your brow hair keeps growing normally throughout the entire process.
