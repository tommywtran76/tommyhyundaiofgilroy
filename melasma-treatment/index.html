01 — The Craft

The condition that punishes
impatience.

Melasma cannot be blasted away — heat and harsh treatment famously make it rebound darker. Our program works with the condition instead: barrier-calming treatments, gentle brightening actives, and disciplined sun defense, sequenced over weeks.

Because melasma is hormonal, honesty is part of the treatment: results are managed, not cured, and maintenance matters. Clients who follow the program see patches soften dramatically — and learn exactly how to keep them quiet.

03 — Questions

Asked often,
answered honestly.

Melasma is managed, not cured — any clinic promising permanent erasure is overpromising. At Aileen's Beauty in Campbell, CA, a patient non-laser program softens patches dramatically and teaches you to keep them suppressed long-term.

Heat is a primary melasma trigger — aggressive laser frequently causes rebound pigmentation that returns darker. A heat-free program of barrier repair, gentle brightening, and strict photoprotection is the safer, evidence-aligned route.

Most clients see visible softening within six to ten weeks of the sequenced program. Improvement continues over months, and maintenance keeps results through summer and hormonal changes.

Hormones (pregnancy, contraception), sun and visible light, and heat — including hot yoga and saunas. Your consultation identifies your personal triggers, because managing them is half the treatment.
