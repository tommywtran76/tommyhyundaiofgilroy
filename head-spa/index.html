01 — The Craft

The scalp is skin.
Treat it that way.

We give our faces ten steps and our scalps a hurried shampoo. Head spa therapy corrects the imbalance: analysis of the scalp, a warm-water cascade, deep exfoliating cleanse, and a long pressure-point massage from crown to neck to shoulders.

The effect is double — healthier roots and visibly glossier hair over time, and a nervous-system calm that clients compare to the deepest hour of a massage. Many fall asleep. That is considered a good outcome.

03 — Questions

Asked often,
answered honestly.

Head spa therapy at Aileen's Beauty in Campbell, CA is a Japanese-inspired scalp treatment: scalp analysis, warm-water cleansing, exfoliation, and an extended pressure-point massage of the head, neck, and shoulders — for scalp health and deep relaxation.

Regular sessions remove buildup that shampoo misses, support healthier hair growth at the root, ease tension headaches, and deliver profound relaxation. Clients report glossier hair and better sleep after a single visit.

Monthly is ideal for scalp health; every two to three weeks if you use heavy styling products or experience tension headaches. As pure stress relief, whenever the week demands it.

Your hair is gently towel-dried and softly finished — plan for a relaxed natural look rather than a blowout. Many clients book head spa on quiet days for exactly this reason.
