01 — The Craft

The color you had
all along.

The goal of lip blush is not lipstick — it is the memory of your natural lip color, returned. Pigment is matched to your undertone and layered in translucent passes, so the healed result is a soft flush that looks like you, rested.

The nano technique uses a single fine needle rather than a blade, which means gentler work, faster healing, and color that fades evenly. A collagen-infused finish leaves lips conditioned, not chapped.

02 — Your Visit

What to expect,
honestly.

Day 0

Consultation & color design

Undertone analysis and a shade conversation in natural light. Numbing is generous; the appointment itself is calm and around two hours.

Days 1–7

The soft-fade week

Color appears bright for the first two days, then softens by roughly half as lips heal — this is designed for, not a surprise.

Weeks 6–8

The perfecting session

Once healed, a refinement visit evens any area that shed lighter and settles your final, everyday flush.

03 — Questions

Asked often,
answered honestly.

Nano collagen lip blush is a semi-permanent lip tint performed at Aileen's Beauty in Campbell, CA with a single fine nano needle, finished with a collagen infusion. It restores soft natural color, blurs uneven pigment, and defines the lip line without a harsh edge.

Healed lip blush typically lasts 2 to 4 years, softening gradually. Sun exposure, smoking, and exfoliating acids shorten wear. A single refresh session revives the color when it fades.

Lips are numbed before and throughout the session, and the nano needle is far gentler than traditional lip tattooing. Most clients rate it easier than they expected — closer to tingling than pain.
